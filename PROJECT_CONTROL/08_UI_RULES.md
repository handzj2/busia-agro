UI RULES
========

1. Follow Product DNA strictly (see 02_PRODUCT_DNA.md).

2. Record-rule pattern (thin top border + date/location) is reserved for genuine dated records (Field Activities, Projects). Do not apply it decoratively to other sections.

3. One primary accent color does the loud work (`harvest`). One secondary does quiet structural work (`clay`). Do not introduce additional bright accent colors without approval.

4. Motion is restrained:
   - Hero entrance animation only on the homepage.
   - No per-section scroll reveals.
   - Card hover lift is consistent.
   - WhatsApp pulse is finite, not infinite.
   - Always respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

5. Do not add decorative icons next to every heading or list item.

6. Do not use generic SaaS card grids, glassmorphism, or heavy gradients.

7. Forms should be efficient and clear. Contact form is intentionally simple.

8. Navigation must remain usable on mobile (existing drawer pattern).

9. When designing future admin UI (V2), do not force the public-site visual language onto dense data screens.
