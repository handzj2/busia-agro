BUSINESS RULES
==============

1. Never invent or display unconfirmed claims.
   - Impact numbers, years of service, certifications, registration status, and trust signals must be confirmed with the client before appearing on the live site.

2. Product and service descriptions must reflect what the shop actually stocks and does.
   - Do not invent product lines or services.

3. Contact channels are primary.
   - Phone and WhatsApp are the preferred contact methods for farmers.
   - The contact form is secondary and optional until Formspree (or equivalent) is configured.

4. Field Activities and Projects are evidence, not marketing copy.
   - Prefer real dates, locations, partners, and results.
   - Do not fabricate activity records.

5. Pricing is not shown in V1 unless explicitly requested and approved.
   - Availability status is shown; prices are handled at the counter / via WhatsApp.

6. The site must remain usable on low-end mobile devices and slower connections common in the region.

7. No hard-sell language. Advice-first tone matches the shop's positioning.
