PRODUCT DNA
===========

PRODUCT CHARACTER
-----------------

The product should feel:

- Credible and grounded (real field presence, not marketing fluff)
- Professional enough for funders and partners
- Straightforward and helpful for farmers
- Specific to Eastern Uganda / Mbale agro-inputs context
- Editorial rather than corporate or startup-y


DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
-----------------

Treat real dates, locations, partners, and results as structural content — not decoration.

The site is an **editorial field-record aesthetic**. Specificity builds trust.

Avoid both "small shop flyer" and "generic SaaS startup" looks.


INFORMATION PHILOSOPHY
----------------------

Most important information:

1. How to contact the shop right now (phone + WhatsApp)
2. What is stocked and available
3. Evidence of real field activity (dates + locations)
4. Company credibility signals that have been verified

What users should see first:

- Clear statement of who the company is and where it is
- Immediate path to contact
- Latest or featured field evidence

What should require the fewest steps:

- Calling or WhatsApping
- Finding a specific product category
- Reading a field activity or project record


WORKFLOW PHILOSOPHY
-------------------

Primary farmer workflow:
Browse → understand product / service → contact (WhatsApp preferred)

Partner / funder workflow:
See evidence of field work and projects → evaluate credibility → contact

The site supports both without forcing one audience into the other's language.


VISUAL IDENTITY
---------------

Primary colors:
- ink          `#16241A`   (headings, dark sections)
- field        `#1F4D2B`   (nav, dark backgrounds)
- harvest      `#E0A526`   (primary CTA accent)
- clay         `#9C4A24`   (secondary accent — dates, eyebrows)
- parchment    `#F3ECDD`   (alternate section background)
- paper        `#FCFAF4`   (page background)
- stone        `#5C5646`   (body text)

Typography:
- Headlines: Fraunces (serif)
- Body / UI: Work Sans

Icon style:
- Minimal. One purposeful leaf mark used at two scales. No decorative icon scatter.

Density:
- Comfortable reading density. Not sparse "startup whitespace". Not cramped.

Shape language:
- Soft but purposeful. Record-rule (thin top border + date/location) reserved for genuine dated content only.


AI DEFAULTS TO AVOID
--------------------

Do not automatically use:

- generic SaaS dashboards
- excessive cards
- unnecessary gradients
- glassmorphism
- decorative illustrations
- meaningless charts
- excessive rounded containers
- excessive whitespace
- generic terminology ("solutions", "leverage", "empower")
- unnecessary animations / scroll reveals on every section
- decorative icons next to every heading
- invented statistics
- the common cream + terracotta AI palette


PREFERRED PATTERNS
------------------

Prefer:

- domain-specific workflows (farmer contact, field records)
- task-oriented screens
- meaningful tables or structured records where appropriate
- clear information hierarchy
- actionable exceptions
- efficient forms
- role-specific interfaces (future admin vs public)
- purposeful visual hierarchy
- meaningful status indicators (availability, project status)
- the existing record-rule pattern for dated content


DESIGN TEST
-----------

Ask:

Could this screen belong to any random SaaS product?

If yes, review the design.

The objective is not to make the interface strange.
The objective is to make the interface purposeful and product-specific.
