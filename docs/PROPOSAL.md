## The proposition

We'll start with a professional website that introduces the business,
showcases products, field activities, and projects, and gives customers
and potential partners a place to learn about the company. We don't need
to build everything at once — as the business grows and needs things like
uploading activities directly, managing projects, tenders, or funding
information, we add those features gradually.

This keeps the initial cost reasonable and lets the site grow in step with
the business, rather than paying upfront for infrastructure that isn't
needed yet.

## What Phase 1 delivers

A professional company website with:

- **Home** — an overview that communicates who you are, what you do, and
  why you're credible, with a path into every other section
- **About Us** — your mission, vision, values, and background
- **Services** — the services you actually provide
- **Products** — a browsable catalogue by category, with enquiry contact
  (no online payment in Phase 1)
- **Field Activities** — a public record of training sessions, farm
  visits, and other field work, each with photos and results
- **Projects** — a portfolio of past and current project work, written to
  support future tender, partnership, and funding conversations
- **Contact** — phone, WhatsApp, email, address, map, and a contact form

Built to be fast, mobile-friendly, and easy to find on Google.

## What Phase 1 deliberately does not include (yet)

- A login or admin area for you to edit content yourself — for Phase 1, you
  send us updates and we publish them
- A database or backend system
- Online ordering or payments
- Tender or funding-opportunity management tools

These are all part of the plan for later phases (below) — Phase 1 is built
so none of this requires starting over.

## What comes later

**Phase 2 — Self-service website.** Once you're regularly adding new field
activities or products, we add a private admin area where you can publish
these yourself — no developer needed for routine updates.

**Phase 3 — Business platform.** Once you're actively managing bids,
tenders, or funding relationships, we add dedicated tools for tracking
opportunities, documents, and partner communication.

Each phase builds on the same foundation — nothing from Phase 1 gets
thrown away or rebuilt.
